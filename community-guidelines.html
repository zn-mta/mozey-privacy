Mozey Community Guidelines

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Mozey is a place to share honest recommendations with people you trust. These guidelines explain what belongs on Mozey and what doesn't. They apply to everything you post, including reviews, comments, photos, tags, your username, your profile photo, and your bio. They are part of our Terms of Use.

Share genuine experiences

  • Review places you've actually experienced. Your reviews should reflect your own visit and your honest opinion.
  • Be helpful. Describe what you liked or didn't, so others can decide for themselves. Criticism is welcome; abuse is not.
  • Be transparent. Don't post reviews you were paid or rewarded to write, and don't review a business you own or work for without saying so.
  • Use photos you have the right to share, and be thoughtful about photographing other people.

Not allowed on Mozey

  • Spam or scams. Repetitive, irrelevant, or promotional content; fake reviews; misleading links; attempts to trick people.
  • Harassment or bullying. Insulting, demeaning, or targeting someone; encouraging others to do so; repeatedly contacting someone who doesn't want it.
  • Hate speech. Attacking or demeaning people based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, caste, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, disability, or serious illness.
  • Sexual content. Nudity or sexually explicit material. Any content that sexualizes minors is strictly prohibited.
  • Violence or threats. Threatening, promoting, or glorifying violence against anyone, or encouraging self-harm.
  • Private information. Sharing someone's personal information without permission, such as a phone number, home address, private messages, or identifying photos.
  • Impersonation. Pretending to be another person or organization, or using a misleading username or profile.
  • Anything illegal or dangerous, including content that promotes illegal activity or infringes someone else's rights.

Reporting and blocking

  • Report a review or profile that breaks these guidelines using the ⋯ menu on the review or profile. Mozey doesn't reveal through the app who submitted a report to the person who was reported.
  • Block someone from their profile if you don't want to interact with them. Blocking ends any follow relationship between you and hides their content from you in the app.
  • If you believe someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

What happens when content breaks the rules

We review reports, but a report alone doesn't remove anything. After review, we may remove content, or restrict or suspend accounts, depending on how serious the issue is and whether it's happened before. When we remove a review, it's hidden from everyone and its photos are permanently deleted.

We may update these guidelines as Mozey grows. Questions? Contact mozeyprivacy@gmail.com.
