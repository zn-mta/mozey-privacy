Mozey Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Mozey is a mobile app for discovering places and sharing reviews with the people you follow. This Privacy Policy explains what information Mozey collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies to the Mozey iOS app.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following to operate your account and provide the app's features:

  • Account information: the email address you use to sign in, and a username you choose.
  • Profile information: your username, an optional profile photo, an optional home city, whether your account is private, and onboarding preferences you select.
  • Content you create: reviews you write, including your score, written comments, optional tags, and any photos you attach to a review.
  • Your activity in the app: places you save or mark as favorites or "Want to Go," and the accounts you follow or request to follow.
  • Location: with your permission, your device's location while you are using the app, to show what's near you on the map (see Section 4).

3. How We Use Information

We use the information above to:

  • create and operate your account;
  • show your profile, reviews, and places;
  • power social features such as following and follow requests;
  • display places on a map and help you discover new ones;
  • keep the app working reliably and diagnose crashes (see Section 8).

4. Location Information

If you grant permission, Mozey uses your device's location only while you are using the app to center the map on your surroundings and show nearby places. Mozey requests location as a one-time fix when needed and does not track your location continuously or in the background. Your precise device location is not stored as a location history. If you choose to provide a home city, Mozey stores that city as part of your profile; that is a place you select, not a record of where your device has been. You can change or deny location access at any time in your device's Settings.

5. Photos and User Content

Reviews, comments, tags, and any photos you attach are stored so Mozey can display them in the app. Please only share content you have the right to share, and avoid including sensitive personal information in reviews or photos.

6. Social Features

Mozey lets you follow other people and, if your account is private, approve follow requests. Some basic profile information, such as your username and profile photo, may be visible to other Mozey users. Your reviews and favorite places are shown to others according to your account's privacy setting — for a public account, or to followers you have approved for a private account. Your saved and "Want to Go" places are private to you and are not shown to other users. Following and follow-request relationships are stored to provide these features.

7. Third-Party Services

Mozey relies on the following third-party services to function:

  • Google Firebase provides Mozey's backend infrastructure, including sign-in (Firebase Authentication), the database that stores your account and content (Cloud Firestore), file storage for photos (Firebase Storage), and secure server functions (Cloud Functions).
  • Firebase Crashlytics provides crash and diagnostic reporting (see Section 8). Firebase Analytics collection is disabled in Mozey.
  • Google Maps and Google Places power the maps and place-discovery features.
  • Feedback form: the in-app "Send Feedback" link opens an external Google Form in your browser. Mozey does not automatically attach your account information to that link; anything you provide in the form is information you choose to enter yourself.

These services process information in connection with providing their functionality to Mozey.

8. Crash Reporting

Mozey uses Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash and diagnostic reports so we can find and fix problems and improve the app's stability. Firebase Analytics collection is disabled in Mozey.

9. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We keep your account information and content while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from Settings in the app. When you do, Mozey's server-side deletion removes your account and its associated content, including your profile information, the reviews you wrote, your saved and favorite places, your following and follow-request relationships, photos you uploaded, your username reservation, and your sign-in credentials.

10. Data Security

We use reputable infrastructure providers and reasonable technical measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at mozeyprivacy@gmail.com.
