Mozey Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Mozey is a mobile app for discovering places and sharing reviews with the people you follow. This Privacy Policy explains what information Mozey collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies to the Mozey iOS app. Mozey is intended for people 18 and older.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following to operate your account and provide the app's features:

  • Account information: the email address you use to sign in, and a username you choose. If you use Sign in with Apple, we receive the email address Apple shares with us, which may be a private relay address if you choose to hide your email. Mozey does not request your name from Apple.
  • Profile information: your username, an optional profile photo, an optional short bio, an optional home city, whether your account is private, and onboarding preferences you select.
  • Content you create: reviews you write, including your score, written comments, optional tags, an optional price tier for hotel stays, and any photos you attach to a review.
  • Your activity in the app: places you save or mark as favorites or "Want to Go," and the accounts you follow or request to follow.
  • Reports you submit: if you report a review or profile, we record the report as described in Section 7.
  • Blocks: the accounts you block.
  • Location: with your permission, your device's location while you are using the app, to show what's near you on the map (see Section 4).

3. How We Use Information

We use the information above to:

  • create and operate your account;
  • show your profile, reviews, and places;
  • power social features such as following and follow requests;
  • review reports, enforce our Terms of Use and Community Guidelines, and help keep Mozey safe;
  • display places on a map and help you discover new ones;
  • understand how the app is used so we can improve it (see Section 8);
  • keep the app working reliably and diagnose crashes (see Section 9).

4. Location Information

If you grant permission, Mozey uses your device's location only while you are using the app to center the map on your surroundings and show nearby places. Mozey requests location as a one-time fix when needed and does not track your location continuously or in the background. Your precise device location is not stored as a location history. Mozey's map is provided by Google Maps, so when location access is on, your location is shown on and processed by the Google Maps map under the Google Privacy Policy. If you choose to provide a home city, Mozey stores that city as part of your profile; that is a place you select, not a record of where your device has been. You can change or deny location access at any time in your device's Settings.

Separately, if you turn on Photo Helper, Mozey uses the dates and locations stored with photos in your library, on your iPhone, to suggest photos for your reviews. See Section 5A.

5. Photos and User Content

Reviews, comments, tags, and any photos you attach are stored so Mozey can display them in the app. Before a photo you attach to a review is uploaded, Mozey removes GPS location information from the photo file. A photo's contents, and the place your review is about, may still reveal where it was taken. Please only share content you have the right to share, and avoid including sensitive personal information in reviews or photos. Content that violates our Terms of Use or Community Guidelines may be removed (see Section 7).

5A. Photo Helper

Photo Helper is an optional feature that suggests photos from your iPhone's photo library when you write a review. It is off until you turn it on, and Mozey asks for access to your Photos only after you choose to use it. You can still add photos to a review with the standard photo picker or your camera without using Photo Helper.

How matching works. When Photo Helper is on, Mozey reads the dates and locations stored with the photos in your library and compares them with the location of the place you are reviewing. Mozey uses the place location it has already received from Google Places when you search for or select that place. Matching happens on your iPhone. Your photos' dates and locations, and the list of suggested photos, are not uploaded to Mozey's servers or sent to Google.

Previews and iCloud. If your photos are stored in iCloud, iOS may download a photo from iCloud when you open it in a full-screen preview or select it for your review.

What is uploaded. Only the photos you choose to include in a review are uploaded to Mozey, when you post the review. Before upload, Mozey removes GPS location information from the photo file. The contents of a photo, such as a recognizable landmark or sign, can still reveal where it was taken, and your review shows the place you reviewed. Uploaded photos are handled as described in Section 5.

Shared Albums and Shared Libraries. If you give Mozey full access to your Photos, you can choose to include photos from Shared Albums in your suggestions. This is off by default and is not available with limited access. If you use an iCloud Shared Photo Library, it may contain photos added by other people, and Photo Helper may suggest them along with your own. Please only post photos you have the right to share.

Your choices. You can turn Photo Helper off at any time in Mozey's Settings. This stops photo suggestions but does not change the Photos permission you gave Mozey in iOS. If you gave Mozey limited access, you can change which photos it can see from Mozey's Settings or your iPhone's Settings app. You can change or remove Mozey's access to your Photos at any time in your iPhone's Settings app.

6. Social Features

Mozey lets you follow other people and, if your account is private, approve follow requests. Some basic profile information, such as your username, profile photo, and bio, is visible to other Mozey users, including when your account is private. Your reviews and favorite places are shown to others according to your account's privacy setting — for a public account, or to followers you have approved for a private account. Your saved and "Want to Go" places are private to you and are not shown to other users. Following and follow-request relationships are stored to provide these features.

You can block another user. Blocking ends any follow relationship between you, prevents either of you from following the other, and hides that person's content from you in the app. Mozey stores a record of the blocks you make. You can see the accounts you've blocked in Settings; the app does not notify the person you block, and they cannot see that record. Blocking does not prevent someone from seeing content that is otherwise publicly available.

7. Reports and Moderation

You can report a review or a profile that you believe violates our Terms of Use or Community Guidelines.

What a report contains. When you submit a report, Mozey stores: your account ID; whether you reported a review or a profile; which review or profile you reported and the account ID of the person who posted it; the reason you selected; any optional details you add (up to 500 characters); the date of the report; and its status. To help us review it, the report also keeps a copy of the reported content as it appeared when you reported it — for a review, its text, score, and tags, and references to its photos (not copies of the photos); for a profile, the username, bio, and a link to the profile photo. To prevent abuse, Mozey also keeps a daily count of the reports each account submits.

How reports are used and who can access them. Reports are used to review the reported content, decide whether to take action, and prevent misuse of reporting. Reports are not visible to other users. They may be accessed by authorized Mozey personnel and by service providers that host and operate Mozey, for moderation and operations. Mozey does not reveal through the app who submitted a report to the person who was reported, but we may disclose information when required by law. Submitting a report may generate an operational notification to Mozey containing limited metadata, such as a report reference number, but not the report's contents.

What we may do. A report does not automatically remove anything; we decide after review. If content violates our rules, we may remove it. When we remove a review, it is hidden from all users and its photos are permanently deleted; its text and score may remain stored with the author's account until that account is deleted. If we remove a bio, it is deleted from the profile, and a copy remains in the related report.

How long we keep reports. Reports that are still under review are kept until they are resolved, which may be longer than one year. Resolved reports become eligible for deletion one year after they were submitted and are deleted during our periodic cleanup, so they may be kept somewhat longer than one year. Daily report counts become eligible for deletion after 30 days and are also removed during periodic cleanup.

If an account is deleted. When an account is deleted, reports about that account are deleted. Reports that account submitted are kept so we can finish reviewing them, but the reporter's account ID and any optional details they wrote are removed. Records of blocks made by or against that account are deleted, and its daily report count is deleted.

8. Third-Party Services

Mozey relies on the following third-party services to function:

  • Google Firebase provides Mozey's backend infrastructure: sign-in (Firebase Authentication); the database that stores your account, content, reports, and blocks (Cloud Firestore); file storage for photos (Firebase Storage); and secure server functions that carry out actions such as following, blocking, recording reports, and deleting accounts (Cloud Functions). Google Cloud Logging and Cloud Monitoring keep operational logs used to run and troubleshoot those functions, and can send Mozey operational notifications, for example when a report is submitted; those notifications contain limited metadata, such as a report reference number, not the report's contents.
  • Firebase Crashlytics provides crash and diagnostic reporting (see Section 9).
  • Google Analytics for Firebase helps us understand how Mozey is used so we can improve it. It collects app usage information, such as which screens and features are used and for how long, along with app and device information, an app-instance identifier, and approximate location derived from your IP address. We do not send your Mozey user ID, username, email address, review text, or the places you view to Google Analytics, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Google Maps and Google Places power the maps and place-discovery features.
  • Feedback form: the in-app "Send Feedback" link opens an external Google Form in your browser. Mozey does not automatically attach your account information to that link; anything you provide in the form is information you choose to enter yourself.

These services process information in connection with providing their functionality to Mozey.

9. Crash Reporting

Mozey uses Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash and diagnostic reports so we can find and fix problems and improve the app's stability.

10. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We keep your account information and content while your account is active. Reports and blocks are kept as described in Section 7. You can delete your account at any time from Settings in the app. When you do, Mozey's server-side deletion removes your account and its associated content, including your profile information, the reviews you wrote, your saved and favorite places, your following and follow-request relationships, blocks you made and blocks of your account, reports about your account (reports you submitted are kept without your account ID or the details you wrote, as described in Section 7), photos you uploaded, your username reservation, and your sign-in credentials. If your account uses Sign in with Apple, Mozey asks you to confirm with Apple and attempts to revoke Mozey's Sign in with Apple authorization before deleting your account. If that cannot be completed, the app tells you and nothing is deleted unless you choose to continue; if you continue, your Mozey account is still deleted, and you can remove Mozey in your Apple Account's Sign in with Apple settings.

Photo Helper settings are stored only on your iPhone. When you delete your account in the app, Mozey removes that account's Photo Helper settings from that iPhone. Deleting your account does not change the Photos permission you gave Mozey in iOS; you can change or remove it in your iPhone's Settings app.

11. Data Security

We use reputable infrastructure providers and reasonable technical measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at mozeyprivacy@gmail.com.
